# Meetings Directory

Records of specific meetings with analysis.

## What goes here

- Meeting records with specific date and attendees
- Calls with transcripts or notes
- Video conferences

## What does NOT go here

- Recurring meeting series → `org/` (as process)
- Meeting preparation notes → `projects/` (as prep work)

## Naming convention

- `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md` — e.g., `2026-05-20-product-review.md`
- Use date, not meeting name, as primary identifier

## Page structure

See `templates/meeting.md` for the full template.

Key sections:
- **Attendees:** With links to people pages
- **Key Decisions:** What was decided
- **Action Items:** With owners
- **Analysis:** Your interpretation

## After creating a meeting page

1. Enrich each attendee's person page
2. Enrich each company discussed
3. Extract action items to tasks
4. Cross-reference all entities
