# Writing

Essays, drafts, and developed prose. Content meant to be read.

## Purpose

Store written content:
- Blog posts
- Essays
- Newsletters
- Long-form content
- Draft publications

## What Goes Here

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Blog posts | Technical articles, opinions |
| Essays | Long-form explorations |
| Newsletters | Regular publications |
| Drafts | Work-in-progress writing |

## Writing vs Concepts

| Writing | Concepts |
|---------|----------|
| Developed prose | Distilled knowledge |
| 500+ words | ~200 words |
| Narrative form | Structured bullets |
| "Here's a story..." | "Here's a framework..." |

## Writing vs Media

| Writing | Media |
|---------|-------|
| The content itself | Distribution strategy |
| What you say | Where you publish |
| The artifact | The channel |
