# Companies Directory

One page per organization. Companies, funds, nonprofits, government bodies.

## What goes here

- For-profit companies
- Venture capital and private equity funds
- Nonprofit organizations
- Government agencies

## What does NOT go here

- People → `people/`
- Products → `projects/` (if being built) or `concepts/` (as category)
- Industries → `concepts/` (as frameworks)

## Naming convention

- `company-name.md` — e.g., `acme-corp.md`
- Use lowercase, hyphens for spaces

## Page structure

See `templates/company.md` for the full template.

Key sections:
- **State:** What, stage, headquarters
- **Business Model:** How they make money
- **Team:** CEO, founders, key hires
- **Funding:** Round history
- **Why They Win:** Competitive advantage
- **Risks:** Key concerns

## Relationship to people

A company page should link to people pages for key individuals:
- CEO → `[[people/ceo-name]]`
- Founders → `[[people/founder-name]]`

The people pages link back.
