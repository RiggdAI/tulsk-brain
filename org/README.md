# Org

Organization strategy, operations, and structure. Knowledge *about* the org.

## Purpose

Document organizational knowledge:
- Strategy and vision
- Operating principles
- Team structure
- Processes and systems

## What Goes Here

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Strategy | Annual plans, OKRs |
| Structure | Team maps, reporting lines |
| Processes | Hiring, onboarding, decision-making |
| Culture | Values, rituals, norms |

## Org vs Programs

- **Org**: Knowledge *about* the organization
- **Programs**: Your role and commitments within it

## Org vs Projects

- **Org**: Enduring systems and structure
- **Projects**: Time-bounded initiatives
